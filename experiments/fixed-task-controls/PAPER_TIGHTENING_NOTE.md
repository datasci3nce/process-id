2. **Real Wiki edge-results paragraph:** inserted a direct p=100% no-deletion control: exactly the same graph yields 65/199 two-hop and 103/199 shared-in-neighbor ranks changed across arms, because candidate sampling differs. Kept actual original scores untouched.

The revised manuscript retains six pages, existing figures and references, the original Wiki/SCALE/CE-free scope, and the pre-existing research lineage. This does not certify compliance with Apart's official mandatory template, which was not available for verification.

## Why DELAY-PERC and CE controls are not inserted into this manuscript

These are additional analyses conducted after the original sprint paper was finalized and are **not** a uniform empirical evaluation of the same real-Wiki inference problem. The delayed-network study supplies an illustrative procedural next step; CE remains a conceptual distinction. Incorporating both as headline results would dilute the bounded real-record result and invite misleading claims about real-agent dynamics. Cite the separate supplemental audit if desired; do not present it as prior preregistration or as the core paper's contribution.

## Reviewer-ready replacement statement

> On the derived Wiki graph, the observed advantage of shared in-neighbor ranking under source-correlated deletion is not an isolated percolation effect. At full retention, where both masking arms have identical graph evidence, independent and group-arm candidate sampling changes the withheld target rank in 65/199 two-hop and 103/199 shared-in-neighbor evaluations. Thus candidate-set changes and eligibility effects must be controlled by a fixed-candidate rerun before attributing ranking changes to evidence deletion. The exact parity experiment separately demonstrates claim-level evidence reliability under a complete declared mechanism class.
